YOUNG MINDS BIBLE — FULL CHAPTER PREVIEW

1. Double-click OFFLINE_PREVIEW.html.
2. While connected to the internet once, tap “Download Full Bible Offline”.
3. Wait until it says all 66 books are saved.
4. You can then disconnect the internet and read Genesis through Revelation from this browser preview.
5. Tap Bible > choose a book > choose a chapter to see the complete chapter verses.

The Android/Play Store build is designed to package the Bible data with the app so end users do not need this browser-download step.
